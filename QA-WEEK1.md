# LIPPS Epidemiology Week 1 — QA

## Scope

Week 1 is intentionally foundational. It uses CDC Lesson One to strengthen epidemiological thinking without absorbing the detailed content reserved for later LIPPS weeks.

## Source alignment

Included from the CDC lesson:

- definition and components of epidemiology;
- distribution as frequency and pattern;
- person, place and time framing;
- population versus clinical perspective;
- historical development through Hippocrates, Graunt, Farr and Snow;
- uses of epidemiology;
- core epidemiologic functions;
- case-definition and comparability logic;
- descriptive versus analytic reasoning;
- importance of a valid comparison group; and
- count, divide and compare as the basic epidemiologic approach.

Deferred to later LIPPS weeks despite appearing in CDC Lesson One:

- detailed descriptive epidemiology;
- detailed study-design classification;
- disease-causation models and epidemiologic triad;
- natural history and spectrum of disease;
- chain of infection; and
- epidemic disease occurrence.

This avoids overloading Week 1 and preserves the 12-week LIPPS curriculum sequence.
