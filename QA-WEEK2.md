# LIPPS Epidemiology Week 2 — QA

## Scope

Week 2 focuses on measuring disease frequency and population health. It is intentionally separated from Week 3, which will handle measures of association and effect.

## Source alignment

Included from CDC Lesson Three:

- ratios, proportions and rates;
- numerator/denominator logic;
- population at risk;
- incidence proportion / risk / attack rate;
- secondary attack rate;
- incidence rate and person-time;
- point and period prevalence;
- the influence of incidence and duration on prevalence;
- crude, cause-specific and stratum-specific mortality;
- age-adjustment as a conceptual warning for crude comparisons;
- case fatality and proportionate mortality;
- neonatal, postneonatal, infant and maternal mortality indicators;
- natality measures; and
- YPLL as an extension concept.

Deferred to Week 3 despite appearing in CDC Lesson Three:

- risk ratio;
- rate ratio;
- odds ratio;
- proportionate mortality ratio as a comparative measure;
- attributable proportion; and
- vaccine efficacy/effectiveness as measures of public-health impact.

This preserves the LIPPS sequence: **Week 2 asks how often an outcome occurs; Week 3 asks how occurrence differs between groups.**
