WHY LIPPS?

Reason beyond the output.

Evidence is always observed through something: a sample, a measurement, a record, a model or an interpretation. What we observe may therefore not perfectly represent what actually happened.

The name LIPPS is inspired by the Signor–Lipps effect in palaeontology—a reminder that an incomplete record can produce a misleading picture of reality.

Research faces the same challenge. Samples are incomplete. Measurements contain error. Records are missing. Associations can mislead. Context matters.

LIPPS exists to help people reason beyond the output.
