## Pedagogical QA decisions

1. The course progresses from foundations → theory → audience → interpersonal/community → message/channel → digital/emergency information environments → campaign evaluation → ethics/capstone.
2. Every week opens with a realistic problem, teaches concepts, applies them to an integrated scenario, and ends with practice plus a timed mastery specification.
3. The banks are deliberately larger than the quiz delivered to any student, but the **current bank is not yet predominantly scenario-oriented**. A substantial proportion consists of repeated concept-recognition templates. These are suitable for practice and retrieval but should not dominate mastery or formal assessment.
4. Timing is a pilot parameter, not an accessibility restriction. Approved accommodations must remain available.
5. Copy/paste is not treated as the primary integrity control. Random banks, scenario-based reasoning, shuffled options and short calibrated windows are the main design choices.
6. Health Communication is not reduced to mass messaging: service barriers, trust, power, community engagement, ethics, accessibility and evaluation are embedded throughout.
7. Risk communication, RCCE and infodemic management are aligned with current WHO framing.
8. The final week integrates the course through **LIPPS SIGNAL**: Situation; Intended audience and insight; Goal; Narrative and needed action; Access; Listen and learn.

## Important limitation

The Moodle banks are **candidate teaching and pilot-assessment items**, not yet psychometrically validated high-stakes exam banks. After student use, analyse item difficulty, discrimination, distractor performance, response times and differential performance before institutional high-stakes deployment.


## University-readiness correction (v1.8)

A deeper audit found that raw item count overstated assessment maturity. Many concepts were represented by three close paraphrases of the same recognition task. Before institutional assessment use, split the bank into foundation/practice, mastery and protected assessment pools and rewrite the latter two toward novel application scenarios.
